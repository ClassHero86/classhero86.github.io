班級英雄 ClassHero ("ClassHero", "we", "us") is a classroom gamification web app for teachers, operated by KTH Studio, an independent software developer based in Hong Kong (班級英雄（由香港獨立開發者 KTH Studio 營運）). You can reach us at riceisgd@hotmail.com.

2. What the service is

ClassHero lets teachers turn classroom performance into in-game progress: student characters, seating-chart points, boss battles, a world map, pet gacha, an auction house and mini-games. It requires a teacher account and stores class data on the teacher's behalf.
